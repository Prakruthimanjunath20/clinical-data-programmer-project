# Closeout and database-lock checklist (training example)

A database lock is a controlled milestone, not just a button. In a real study, follow the authorised study SOP and platform procedure.

Before requesting lock, the study team would normally confirm the study-specific requirements, which may include:

- Expected visits and forms have been reviewed for completeness.
- Open data queries and discrepancies have been reviewed and resolved or formally dispositioned under procedure.
- Required forms/casebooks have the required review/sign-off status.
- Data transfers and reconciliation checks have been completed and documented.
- The final export/listings have been checked and approved by the responsible team.
- Access, unblinding restrictions and permissions have been reviewed.
- Required study documents, specifications, test evidence and decisions are filed.
- The appropriate authorised people approve database lock.
- The final extract and archival records are retained securely and can be interpreted later.

After lock, data access/change permissions depend on the system and SOP. Do not assume locked data can be freely edited; corrections after lock require the formal authorised process.

## In this project
Use `outputs/validation_issues.csv` as a mock pre-lock review list.
